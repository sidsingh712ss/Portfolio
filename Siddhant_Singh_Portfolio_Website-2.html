Selected work

Case 01

Running paid + organic marketing for 3 clients, 3 industries, at once

Digital Marketing Intern — Idecution Marketing LLP

Situation

Three clients — real estate, education, and manufacturing — needed paid campaigns, organic social, and content running at once, with no shared playbook and client communication resting on me directly.

Approach

Ran Meta & Google Ads and optimized organic social per client; wrote ad copy, captions, and newsletters for LinkedIn and websites; built KPI dashboards; and stayed the direct point of contact for client communication.

Result

+30% lead volume and +18% CTR, plus client relationships I managed directly end-to-end and a repeatable process each client could keep using.

Case 02

Social Media & Content Volunteer — Ahbee Agency (Remote)

Situation

A fully remote, international team needed a consistent content presence on Instagram and LinkedIn, without an existing calendar or process.

Approach

Planned and ran a content calendar, designed 20+ on-brand visual assets in Canva, and tracked engagement trends to see what actually landed.

Result

A consistent, on-brand publishing rhythm, and a habit of feeding engagement data back into what got planned next.

Case 03
Independent research · public data only

Benchmarking Allianz Germany against ERGO and AXA — with zero internal access

Self-directed competitive analysis — September 2026

Situation

Wanted to test whether real competitive intelligence was possible with no internal dashboards, no paid tools, and no insider access — only what any customer scrolling a feed could see.

Approach

Reviewed 18 ads via the public Meta Ad Library and several months of organic Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn activity (May–Aug 2026) across all three brands, reading creative choices, posting cadence, tone, and comment engagement in place of metrics I couldn't access.

Result

Surfaced 5 concrete findings and 3 testable recommendations — including an under-used content pillar with the strongest engagement of any post reviewed — packaged as an 11-slide research deck and LinkedIn article.
