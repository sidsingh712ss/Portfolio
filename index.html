Insurance · Competitive Intelligence
Benchmarking Allianz Germany vs. ERGO & AXA — Using Only Public Data
3 Brands Benchmarked
18 Ads Reviewed
13.2K Peak Post Likes Found
The Question

Could I produce a genuinely useful competitive read on Allianz Germany's social strategy with no internal dashboards, no paid tools, and no insider access — using only what any customer scrolling their feed could see?

What I Did
  • Reviewed 18 individual ads across Allianz, ERGO, and AXA via the public Meta Ad Library
  • Tracked several months of organic Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn activity (May–Aug 2026) for all three brands
  • Read what was actually visible — creative choices, posting cadence, tone of voice, and comment engagement — instead of guessing at metrics I couldn't access
  • Synthesised the findings into 5 key patterns and 3 testable recommendations, ranked by effort vs. impact
The Result

Found that Allianz is the most video-heavy of the three but not the least frequent poster, that Allianz and ERGO both show signs of unmanaged ad duplication, and that AXA runs the most disciplined creative testing yet has the smallest audience. The clearest opportunity: Allianz's FC Bayern collaboration post pulled in 13.2K likes and 159 comments — the strongest engagement of any post reviewed — but runs only once a month instead of as a repeatable content pillar. Packaged as an 11-slide research deck and a LinkedIn article.

Real Estate · Paid Social
20% CPL Reduction for a Property Developer
−20% Cost per Lead
+30% Lead Volume
The Problem

A real estate client was spending heavily on Meta and Google Ads but generating expensive, low-quality leads. CPL was high and conversion to viewings was low.

What I Did
  • Audited the existing campaigns and identified poor audience segmentation and generic ad creative as the main issues
  • Rebuilt the campaign structure with tighter geographic targeting and separate ad sets for different buyer profiles (first-time buyers vs. investors)
  • Wrote and designed new creatives using Canva — specific, benefit-led copy instead of generic property ads
  • Set up A/B tests across 4 creative variants and 3 audience sets simultaneously
  • Built a Google Sheets dashboard to track CPL, CTR, and form completion rate daily
The Result

Within 6 weeks: cost per lead dropped 20%, lead volume grew 30%, and CTR improved 18% on the winning creative. The client renewed the contract.

Education · Multi-Platform
Full-Funnel Campaign for an EdTech Client
+18% CTR
3 Platforms Managed
The Problem

An education client needed enrolment campaigns across Google, Meta, and LinkedIn — three different audience mindsets and three different creatives required, all within the same budget.

What I Did
  • Mapped out a platform strategy: Google Search for intent-based (people already searching courses), Meta for interest-based reach, LinkedIn for professional upskilling angle
  • Wrote tailored copy for each channel and designed creatives per platform specification
  • Set up UTM tracking and a shared reporting dashboard so performance across all three could be compared in one place
  • Ran weekly performance reviews and reallocated budget to best-performing channels
The Result

Google Search delivered the lowest CPL, LinkedIn had the highest course completion rate downstream. Overall CTR improved 18% after creative iteration in week 3. Insights were reported to the client with clear budget recommendations.
